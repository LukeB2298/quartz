# Resources

A collection of recommended resources for psychology students.

## Textbooks

- Eysenck, M. W., & Keane, M. T. (2020). *Cognitive Psychology: A Student's Handbook* (8th ed.). Psychology Press.
- Gross, R. (2020). *Psychology: The Science of Mind and Behaviour* (8th ed.). Hodder Education.
- Field, A. (2024). *Discovering Statistics Using IBM SPSS Statistics* (6th ed.). SAGE.
- Passer, M. W., & Smith, R. E. (2020). *Psychology: The Science of Mind and Behaviour* (3rd ed.). McGraw-Hill.

## Online resources

- [Simply Psychology](https://www.simplypsychology.org) — clear explanations of key topics
- [Khan Academy Psychology](https://www.khanacademy.org) — free video lectures
- [Coursera Psychology Courses](https://www.coursera.org) — university-level online courses
- [APA Style Guide](https://apastyle.apa.org) — citation and formatting standards

## Tools

- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) — note-taking app this site is built with
- [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz) — the engine that powers this website
- [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net) — spaced repetition flashcards
- [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org) — reference management
- [JASP](https://jasp-stats.org) — free, open-source statistics software
