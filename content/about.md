# About This Site

This website is a curated collection of notes from a Bachelor of Science in Psychology program. It is built to help future and current students navigate course material in a structured, linked, and searchable format.

## Purpose

University psychology programs cover a vast range of topics, from neurochemistry to social dynamics. This site aims to:

1. Provide **concise summaries** of key topics for quick review
2. Link related **concepts** together so you can see connections
3. Offer **study guides** for exam preparation
4. Serve as a **reference** that grows over time

## How notes are organized

| Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|
| [[Modules]] | Course modules organized by topic |
| [[Concepts]] | Key theories, terms, and definitions |
| [[Study Guides]] | Exam prep and revision materials |
| [[Research Methods]] | Statistics, experimental design, and tools |

## Technology

This site is built with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz), a static site generator designed for publishing Obsidian vaults. Notes are written in Markdown with Obsidian-style wikilinks.
