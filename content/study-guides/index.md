# Study Guides

This section contains exam preparation materials and revision guides organized by topic.

## Exam preparation tips

1. **Active recall** — test yourself rather than re-reading passively
2. **Spaced repetition** — review material at increasing intervals
3. **Elaborative interrogation** — ask "why" and "how" to deepen understanding
4. **Interleaving** — mix topics rather than studying one at a time
5. **Practice past papers** — familiarize yourself with question formats
