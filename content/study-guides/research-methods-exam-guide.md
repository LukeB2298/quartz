# Research Methods Exam Guide

This guide covers the essential topics for a research methods and statistics exam.

## Experimental design

### Key terms to know
- **Independent variable (IV)** — the variable manipulated
- **Dependent variable (DV)** — the variable measured
- **Confounding variable** — an uncontrolled variable that affects the DV
- **Control variable** — a variable held constant

### Design types
| Design | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| Between-subjects | No order effects | Needs more participants, individual differences |
| Within-subjects | Fewer participants, controls individual differences | Order effects, fatigue |
| Matched pairs | Controls individual differences | Time-consuming matching |

### Controls
- **Random assignment** — distributes participant variables evenly
- **Counterbalancing** (ABBA or Latin square) — controls order effects
- **Placebo control** — isolates treatment effect from expectation

## Statistics

### Choosing the right test
| Data type | 2 groups | 3+ groups | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interval/ratio, normally distributed | Independent t-test / Paired t-test | One-way ANOVA / Repeated measures ANOVA | Pearson's r |
| Ordinal or non-normal | Mann-Whitney U / Wilcoxon | Kruskal-Wallis / Friedman | Spearman's rho |
| Categorical | Chi-square | Chi-square | Cramér's V |

### p-values and significance
- **Alpha (α)** — the threshold for significance, usually .05
- **p-value** — probability of the result if the null hypothesis is true
- **Type I error** — rejecting a true null (false positive)
- **Type II error** — failing to reject a false null (false negative)
- **Power** — 1 minus beta; probability of correctly rejecting a false null

### Effect sizes
- **Cohen's d**: 0.2 = small, 0.5 = medium, 0.8 = large
- **Pearson's r**: 0.1 = small, 0.3 = medium, 0.5 = large
- **Eta-squared**: proportion of variance explained

## Validity and reliability

- **Internal validity** — the study measures what it claims (no confounds)
- **External validity** — results generalize to other settings
- **Ecological validity** — results generalize to real-world settings
- **Construct validity** — measures the intended theoretical construct
- **Test-retest reliability** — consistent over time
- **Inter-rater reliability** — consistent across observers

## Ethics checklist

- [ ] Informed consent obtained
- [ ] Right to withdraw clearly stated
- [ ] No deception, or justified and debriefed
- [ ] Confidentiality and data protection
- [ ] Minimization of harm
- [ ] Ethical approval from review board
