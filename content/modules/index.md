# Course Modules

This section contains notes organized by the main modules of a BSc Psychology program.

## Year 1 — Foundations

- [[Cognitive Psychology]] — perception, attention, memory, and language
- [[Social Psychology]] — attitudes, group behavior, social cognition
- [[Developmental Psychology]] — cognitive and social development across the lifespan
- [[Biological Psychology]] — the brain, nervous system, and behavior

## Year 2 — Intermediate

- [[Research Methods]] — experimental design and statistical analysis
- [[Abnormal Psychology]] — classification and treatment of mental disorders
- [[Personality Psychology]] — trait theories and individual differences
- [[Cognitive Neuroscience]] — brain imaging and cognitive function

## Year 3 — Advanced

- [[Clinical Psychology]] — assessment and intervention
- [[Forensic Psychology]] — psychology and the legal system
- [[Health Psychology]] — behavior, stress, and well-being
